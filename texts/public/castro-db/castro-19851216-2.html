[Text] Commander in Chief Fidel Castro Ruz made the closing remarks at the Seventh International New Latin American Film Festival, which has ended after 14 days in Havana and during which 400 films were shown at various movie theaters. Earlier the Coral Awards were conferred in several film categories. Fidel spoke about how Latin American cinema has been a victim of imperialist rule for many years. He noted the need to get to know better African cinema and that of each country of our continent.

Allow me to tell you that what we have seen during this film festival is so encouraging, so stimulating, that more than ever our belief is confirmed that we will not be culturally colonized forever, we will not be dependent forever, we will not be exploited forever, and that some day, sooner rather than later, our time for freedom will come. Thank you very much.
