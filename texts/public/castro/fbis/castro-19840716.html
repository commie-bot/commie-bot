[Text] Commander in Chief Fidel Castro, first secretary of the party and president of the Councils of State and Ministers, has given the concluding speech at the Pioneers Day ceremony held at the Ernesto Guevara Central Pioneers Palace He said: The children have thanked us for carrying out the revolution and we have thanked them a thousand times for the profound hope they place in us.

In his speech, the commander in chief referred t the enormous effort made by the revolution during the past 25 years. He said: No matter how great the revolution's accomplishments, we have no right to feel satisfied and we will not feel satisfied with what we have done. Eternal impatience must be one of our slogans because when we achieve something, we always know we can still accomplish much more.

Other members of the party Polithuro, leaders of the state, mass organizations, Union of Young Communists [UJC], and different institutions were with Fidel on the speakers platform. Thousands of Pioneers representing all the country's children attended the ceremony. Among them were vanguard members of the Pioneer Organization. The ceremony also commemorated the 10th anniversary of the celebration of Pioneers Day in Cuba, and the fifth anniversary of the opening of the Ernesto Guevara Central Pioneers Palace.
